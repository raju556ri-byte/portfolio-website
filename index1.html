About

I Studied Aerospace Engineering at IOE Pulchowk Campus, Tribhuvan University. As Propulsion, Structure and Fabrication Head of the SRB Club, I took Engines from Thermochemical analysis and CFD to lathe work, composite layup and static fire tests.

My final year project was LUME-X1, the first liquid rocket engine built in Nepal. I also spent three months as a research intern in the Aircraft Propulsion Laboratory at IIT Bombay, working on propellant chemistry and combustion.

Analysis

Mach 4.3 hypersonic intake

Designed a single-ramp intake with oblique shock theory for shock-on-lip conditions. I automated throat sizing and the θ-β-M solutions in Python and reached 0.8466 total pressure recovery.

A 2D RANS simulation in Fluent (k-ω SST) matched the analytical mass flow of 25.85 kg/s within 1.03%.
