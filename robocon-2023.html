ABU Robocon 2023: A Compact Ring-Tossing Robot

Mechanical design and fabrication for Nepal’s robots at the ABU Asia-Pacific Robot Contest 2023. The team reached the quarter-finals, and its compact robot won the Mabuchi Motor Award.

The challenge

The 2023 theme was casting flowers over Angkor Wat, played as a ring toss. Each team builds two cooperating robots, a rabbit robot and an elephant robot, that pick up rubber-hose rings and toss them onto poles at the centre of the field. There are 40 red and blue rings and 11 poles, and the team whose rings are on top at the end scores for each pole. A team that puts its colour on top of all of its poles wins at once. The two robots have different zones and size limits, so how they share the work is the team’s strategy. The rules also cap the combined weight of both robots at 50 kg and require all robots to pack into one transport crate.

My role

I was the team’s Design and Fabrication Head. I designed and machined the mechanical assemblies, including base plates, motor mounts, shafts and bearing housings, from CAD through to integration with the robot. Late in the build the robot had to meet a weight limit. I met it by cutting weight-relief pockets into parts and replacing some metal parts with 3D-printed ones.

The robots

The photos show the features of the robots: omnidirectional wheels for moving in any direction, a pair of rollers that throw the rings, a 3D-printed ring magazine, pneumatic lines, onboard control electronics, a sensor on top, and emergency stop buttons. The frame combines machined metal parts with 3D-printed parts.

Result

Team Nepal reached the quarter-finals of ABU Robocon 2023, where it was drawn against China. The ABU Asia-Pacific Robot Contest Organising Committee presented the Mabuchi Motor Award on 27 August 2023, and the Robotics Club of IOE Pulchowk Campus announced that the robot won it for its compact design.
