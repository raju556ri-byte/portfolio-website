About

I’m an aerospace engineer from Nepal working on experimental and computational rocket propulsion: solid, hybrid and liquid. I led propulsion design and fabrication on LUME-X1, the first known successful liquid rocket engine built by Nepali students, as part of a four-person team, and propulsion, structure and fabrication on a solid-fuel rocket flown to 2.6 km. I also carried out propellant combustion research at IIT Bombay.

My research interests are rocket propulsion, high-speed compressible flows, experimental combustion and data-driven CFD. I’m seeking a funded graduate position.

Featured Systems

LUME-X1 Liquid Engine

Final-year team project: a pressure-fed ethanol/GOX liquid rocket engine, covered by Techpana as the first known successful liquid rocket engine built by Nepali students. The first hot fire succeeded, and across five static fires measured c* efficiency was 0.92–0.94. Read the case study →

Mach 4.3 Supersonic Intake

Designed a single-ramp, shock-on-lip intake with oblique shock theory, automating throat sizing and θ–β–M solutions in Python. Analytical total pressure recovery was 0.8874, and 2D RANS (k–ω SST) gave 0.8466 with mass flow within 1.03% of the design value. Read the case study and try the calculator →

Project Trishul

Led propulsion, structure and fabrication for a two-metre solid-fuel rocket that flew to 2,612 m in January 2025 against a 3,000 m target. The second motor test delivered 3,775 N·s, about 91% of the simulated impulse. Read the case study →

IIT Bombay Propellant Research

Research internship on laser ignition, slurry and gel propellant combustion, electrically controlled propellants and hybrid motor testing. Iron oxide raised the slurry burn-rate coefficient from 1.42 to 1.75 mm/(s·barn). Read the case study →

ABU Robocon 2023

Design and fabrication lead for the Nepal team’s ring-tossing robots in Phnom Penh. The team reached the quarter-finals, and the compact robot won the Mabuchi Motor Award. Read the case study →

Project Bajra: Active-Fin Controlled Rocket

2025 · my contribution to my seniors’ final-year project

Bajra is an integrated control system for stabilizing and guiding a rocket with active fins. I fabricated its lightweight solid rocket motor from glass fiber and polyester resin, precision-machined the graphite nozzle, and prepared the propellant, meeting strict weight and performance constraints for flight testing. The control system and flight computer were developed by the senior team. Read more →
