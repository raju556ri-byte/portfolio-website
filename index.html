About

I’m an aerospace engineer from Nepal working on experimental and computational rocket propulsion: solid, hybrid and liquid. I led propulsion design and fabrication on LUME-X1, the first known successful liquid rocket engine built by Nepali students, as part of a four-person team, and on a national-record solid rocket. I also carried out propellant combustion research at IIT Bombay.

My research interests are rocket propulsion, high-speed compressible flows, experimental combustion and data-driven CFD. I’m seeking a funded graduate position.

Featured Systems

LUME-X1 Liquid Engine

Final-year team project: a pressure-fed ethanol/GOX liquid rocket engine, covered by Techpana as the first known successful liquid rocket engine built by Nepali students. The first hot fire succeeded, and across five static fires measured c* efficiency was 0.92–0.94. Read the case study →

Mach 4.3 Hypersonic Intake

Designed a single-ramp, shock-on-lip intake with oblique shock theory, automating throat sizing and θ–β–M solutions in Python. It reached 0.8466 total pressure recovery, and 2D RANS (k–ω SST) mass flow matched the analytical value within 1.03%.

Project Trishul

Led propulsion, structure and fabrication for a solid-fuel rocket that reached 2.62 km in 2025, setting a Nepal national record.
