Awards and Recognition

Competition wins from 2023 and 2024, with the certificates and announcements behind them.

PRAKRITI ’24 Model Exhibition: first position

PRAKRITI 2024 was organized by the Environmental Engineering students of the Department of Environmental Science and Engineering at Kathmandu University. My entry took first position in the Model Exhibition, and the prize was presented in May 2024.

AIChE K-12 STEM Outreach Competition: winner

The AIChE Student Chapter at IOE ran this competition on 12 February 2024. The certificate recognizes outstanding engagement and demonstration, and I secured the winner position.

Mabuchi Motor Award, ABU Robocon 2023
